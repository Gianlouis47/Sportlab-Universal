# MLB knowledge

Durable MLB methodology and research live here.

- `framework/`: end-to-end analysis, pitcher props, lineups, Statcast, market value, context, validation and learning.
- `methods/`: notebook offense/defense method and pitcher-style profiles.
- `markets/`: sportsbook/provider behavior and market availability notes.

Dynamic lineups, injuries, odds, game logs and simulation results belong in Neon SportLab. Historical StrikeoutLab implementation details belong in `legacy/strikeoutlab/`.
